# Legal and regulatory approval checklist

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. The statutory references below come from the project
brief and **have not been independently verified**. A Nigerian electoral-law practitioner must confirm them.

## Before any public launch (information and demonstration only)

- [ ] Electoral-law opinion confirming an independent information and demonstration platform is permissible,
      and on the wording of disclaimers.
- [ ] Review of the brief's references to the Electoral Act 2026 (accreditation, personal attendance, voting at
      the assigned polling unit, electronic transmission of results).
- [ ] Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: lawful basis, privacy notice, retention schedule, and a Data Protection
      Impact Assessment. Confirm registration duties with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
- [ ] Decision on the Coat of Arms: written permission, or keep the provisional emblem.
- [ ] Permission or licence for party logos and candidate photographs.
- [ ] Terms of use and accessibility statement.
- [ ] Licensed photography to replace the AI-generated images.

## Before using any official data

- [ ] Written INEC authorisation and a data-sharing agreement for results, party and candidate data.
- [ ] Agreed technical interface, source signing and attribution wording.
- [ ] Broadcast standards review if stations will air the graphics.

## Before any binding electoral function (not in scope)

- [ ] Change in law permitting the function, and explicit INEC authorisation.
- [ ] Independent security certification, penetration test, cryptographic review and disaster-recovery test.
- [ ] Controlled, non-binding pilot approved by INEC.

Until every item in the last section is complete, real-election voting stays unimplemented.
